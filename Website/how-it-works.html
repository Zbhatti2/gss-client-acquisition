How the Granite Signal Lead Response System Works

We sell one standardized result: faster, structured handling of your own inbound requests.

We do not sell a menu of random AI services — every client gets the same reliable system, tuned to their business.

What Every Standard Client Receives

Inbound response layer

Immediate acknowledgment for approved web inquiries; missed-call follow-up can be added only after the client's use case and consent process are reviewed.

Qualification

Service, town/ZIP, short problem description, urgency, preferred contact time, and optional photo request.

Routing

Estimate booking where appropriate, otherwise a clean callback task and owner notification.

Guardrails

No binding quote, no unsafe diagnosis, no invented availability, automatic human handoff for emergencies/uncertainty.

CRM visibility

Every inquiry moves through a visible pipeline instead of disappearing in voicemail, email, or memory.

Monitoring

Failure review, opt-out review, transcript sampling, one controlled improvement at a time.

Monthly proof

Response metrics, conversations, bookings/callbacks, handoffs, errors, and changes made.
