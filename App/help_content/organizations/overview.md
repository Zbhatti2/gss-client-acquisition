The Organizations list shows every organization your tenant is tracking —
prospects, clients, and anything in between.

## Searching and filtering

Use the search box to match on organization name, and the dropdown filters
to narrow by Type, Domain, or SubDomain. Choosing a Domain narrows the
SubDomain filter to only the SubDomains that belong to it, so you never
end up picking a combination that can't match anything.

## Adding an organization

Click **New Organization** in the top-right. Only the name is required —
everything else (type, size, domain/subdomain classification, a main
address and contact details) can be filled in now or added later from the
organization's own page.

## Organization Types, Domains & SubDomains

These are picklists, not free text — see **Table Maintenance** if you need
