Only **Organization name** is required. Everything else can be filled in
now or left for later — this form is meant to get an organization into
the system quickly, not to capture everything about it in one sitting.

## Number of Employees & Size Category

Type a number into **Number of Employees** and GSS suggests a matching
**Size Category** (Small, Medium-Small, Medium, Large) automatically. The
suggestion is just a starting point — pick a different category any time,
and your choice sticks even if you later change the employee count.

## Domain & SubDomain

**Domain** is the organization's industry/sector. Once you pick one, the
**SubDomain** dropdown enables and narrows to just the SubDomains that
belong to that Domain — pick Domain first.

## Main contact info

The Street/City/State/Main Phone/Main Email fields on this form are a
single, optional, quick-entry address and contact set — plenty of
prospects don't have this on file yet, so none of it is required. Once
you save, the organization's own page lets you add a **Mailing Address**
and a separate **Physical Address**, multiple phone lines by type, and
documents — the kind of multi-value detail that doesn't fit on a single
quick-add form.

## Editing later

Editing here never overwrites an address that was previously entered as a
single "quick-add" text string (from an import, say) unless you actually
type something into the Street/City/State fields — so it's always safe to
open Edit just to change one field.
