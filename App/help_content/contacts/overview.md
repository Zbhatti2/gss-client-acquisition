The Contacts list shows everyone in your tenant's network — people at
organizations you're tracking, and anyone else worth keeping on file.

## File As vs. Full Name

Contacts sort by **File As** first (falling back to full name), so two
people with the same first name file predictably — set File As explicitly
when a contact goes by something other than "First Last" (e.g. sorting a
company contact under their organization's name).

## Priority contacts

Marking a contact as a priority contact keeps them pinned near the top of
the list — useful for the handful of relationships you're actively
working, out of a much longer list of everyone else on file.
