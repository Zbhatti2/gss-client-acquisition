Customer Service is a placeholder today, not a working module yet. It's
deliberately kept separate from Client Acquisition for what happens
*after* a prospect becomes a client — complaints, support tickets, issues,
delivery tasks, and notifying your delivery team.

Closing an Opportunity as **Close — Won** in Client Acquisition doesn't
hand anything off here automatically yet; that connection is planned but
not built. Check back once this module ships, or ask your GSS
administrator if it's a priority for your organization.
