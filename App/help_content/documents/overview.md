This module is your team's shared library — files, notes, and reference
material, organized so it's easy to find again later.

## Content Type vs. Knowledge Domain

- **Content Type** (Article, Template, Recording, etc.) is a single choice
  per item — what kind of thing it is.
- **Knowledge Domain** is a checklist, not a single pick — one item can
  span more than one domain (e.g. a recording that touches both "Sales
  Enablement" and "Onboarding").

Both are picklists managed from **Utilities > Table Maintenance**.

## Keywords & Hashtags

Type keywords or hashtags as a comma-separated list — GSS splits them into
individual searchable terms and de-duplicates automatically, so it
doesn't matter if the same term gets typed twice across different items.

## Linking to contacts

An item can be linked to one or more contacts (with a link type, e.g.
"Author" or "Subject") — useful for tracing who a piece of content came
from or is about.
