Client Acquisition is GSS's sales pipeline — it tracks a prospective client's
journey from first contact to close as an **Opportunity**. An Opportunity
links to an existing Organization (and its Contacts); there's no separate
"clients" list, since Organizations and Contacts already are your
prospect/client records.

## The dashboard

Four stat tiles orient you at a glance: **Active Opportunities**, **Win
Rate**, **At Risk**, and **Due This Week**. Below them, a **Pipeline
Funnel** chart (filterable by template) shows how many Opportunities sit in
each stage, and **Due This Week** / **At Risk** panels list the tasks and
Opportunities that need attention now.

- **New Opportunity** — start tracking a prospect.
- **All Opportunities** — the full list, filterable.
- **Reporting** — pipeline totals and win-rate breakdowns over time.
- **Pipeline Templates** — manage the stage sequence Opportunities move
  through (see the Pipeline Templates page in this section).

## Creating an Opportunity

You'll pick an existing **Organization** and a **Pipeline Template**
(which stage sequence this prospect follows), and can optionally set an
**Owner / Assigned Rep**, **Opportunity Value**, **Lead Source** (with a
free-text **Lead Source Detail**), and a **Next Action** + date to remind
yourself what's next. Entering the template's first stage automatically
snapshots that stage's checklist and generates any outreach-cadence tasks
it defines — see this section's page on moving an Opportunity through its
pipeline for how that works day to day.

## Opportunities from your website

If your organization has a public marketing website with a contact form
wired up to GSS (see System Management's **Lead Intake Config**),
submissions from it create an Opportunity here automatically — no one on
your team has to enter them by hand. Look for **Lead Source: Website** on
an Opportunity to spot these.
