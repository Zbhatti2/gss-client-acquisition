An Opportunity's page is where you work it day to day — log contact,
check off the current stage's checklist, and move it forward (or not).

## Advancing a stage

**Advance to \<next stage\>** moves the Opportunity forward and copies over
that stage's own checklist and cadence tasks. If the current stage has
**required** checklist items still unchecked, advancing is blocked until
either they're completed or you explicitly override (with a reason) — that
override is recorded on the Opportunity's Stage History, so there's always
a record of why a required step was skipped.

**Move Back** (with a **Confirm Move Back**) reverts to the previous
stage — useful when a deal genuinely regresses, not just a data-entry
fix.

## Nurture, Reactivate, and Lost

- **Nurture** — pause the pipeline without losing your place. Pick a
  reason and, optionally, a revisit date, which creates a **Nurture
  Revisit** task so it doesn't just disappear from view.
- **Reactivate** — resumes an Opportunity from Nurture (or Lost) at
  whatever stage it left off, rather than restarting from stage one.
- **Mark Lost** — a required reason, and a terminal state (use Reactivate
  if it turns out the deal isn't actually dead).

## Closing as Won

**Close — Won** is the only true terminal success state. Customer Service
(the module for what happens after a client signs — support, delivery,
tickets) isn't built yet, so closing here doesn't hand the client off
anywhere automatically today — see this app's Customer Service section.

## Checklist, Tasks, Contacts, and Interactions

- **Stage Checklist** — the current stage's required/optional items,
  copied from the Pipeline Template when the Opportunity entered this
  stage. Editing the template later never rewrites an Opportunity that
  already snapshotted its own copy.
- **Tasks** — some are auto-generated by the template's outreach cadence
  when a stage is entered; add your own anytime. Check one off with the
  checkmark button.
- **Contacts** — link additional people at the Organization to this
  Opportunity, each with a role (Economic Buyer, Champion, Influencer,
  etc.).
- **Log an Interaction** — record a call, email, meeting, or other touch
  point (channel, direction, outcome). This is what risk flags and the
  "days since last contact" clock below are computed from.

## Risk flags

Shown only on **Active** Opportunities (Nurture/Lost/Closed are settled,
not something to chase) — computed live, never manually set:

- **stalled** — no logged interaction in the last 14 days.
- **aging** — longer in the current stage than that stage's typical
  window.
- **ghosted** — the last 3 logged interactions were all outbound with no
  answer (no reply, voicemail only).
- **blocked** — aging *and* still has an incomplete required checklist
  item for the current stage.

If these thresholds don't match how your team actually works, they're a
System Admin / developer-level setting, not something changeable from the
UI today — worth flagging to whoever maintains your GSS install.
