The Agents Library lists GSS's optional AI-powered features — things like
**AI Business Card Import** (Data Exchange) and **AI Ad/Listing Import**
(an Organization's page, and Data Exchange's bulk import) that call an
outside AI service (Anthropic's Claude) to read a photo for you. Because
they call an outside service, they aren't turned on for a tenant
automatically.

## Requesting access

Anyone can see the library and what's already available to their
organization; a **Tenant Admin** is the one who can click **Request
Access**. That sends the request to a System Admin for review — you'll see
it marked "Requested" until they decide.

## What happens after approval

Once approved, the feature just works — the buttons that were locked
(e.g. Data Exchange > Import Business Card) become usable for everyone in
your organization, not just the person who requested it.

## System Admin: reviewing requests

**Agent Requests** (visible only to System Admins) lists every tenant's
request across the whole platform. Approve, deny (with an optional note
explaining why), or revoke an agent a tenant already has — revoking takes
effect immediately.

## Usage

Every successful use of an approved agent is recorded (which tenant, which
agent, when) — this is bookkeeping for a future billing feature, not
something you need to act on today.
