GSS helps you track every organization and contact you're pursuing, the
documents and knowledge you've built up around them, and the people on
your team working the relationship — all in one place, scoped to your
organization.

## Finding your way around

The sidebar on the left is organized around GSS's main modules:

- **Organizations** — the companies you're tracking or working with.
- **Contacts** — the people at those organizations (and anyone else in
  your network).
- **Documents & Knowledge Base** — files, notes, and reference material,
  taggable by domain and linkable to contacts.
- **Utilities** — Data Exchange (import/export), Table Maintenance
  (the picklists used throughout the app), and Geography Maintenance.
- **System Management** — your account, users, audit log, and backups.

## Using this help panel

Click the **?** button in the top-right of almost any screen to open this
panel scoped to what you're looking at. You can always:

- **Browse** the full tree on the left — click a section to expand it.
- **Search** using the box above the tree — it matches page titles and
  keywords as you type.
- Keep working with the panel open — it's a drawer, not a dialog, so your
  form underneath stays exactly as you left it.

If a page doesn't answer your question, that's worth telling your GSS
administrator — this content lives in the app itself and can be expanded
just like everything else here.
