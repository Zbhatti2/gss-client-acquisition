Import Business Card

Upload a photo or scan of a business card. Claude reads the text and pre-fills an Organization and a Contact for you to review and correct before anything is saved. If the card doesn't already exist in the database, new Organization/Contact records are created; if a match is found (by company name, and by the person's email or name), only the fields that are still blank on the existing record get filled in — nothing already on file is overwritten.

Only if the back has printed info worth capturing (a second office, a QR code caption, etc.).
